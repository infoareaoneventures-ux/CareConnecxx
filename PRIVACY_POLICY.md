## 1. INTRODUCTION

CareConnex ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the confidentiality of your personal and health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, website, and services (collectively, the "Services").

**This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:**
- Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
- Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act (HITECH)
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
- California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
- Other applicable state and federal privacy laws

---

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Personal Information
We may collect the following categories of personal information:

**From Clients/Seniors:**
- Name, address, phone number, email address
- Date of birth, age, gender
- Emergency contact information
- Health information and medical history
- Care needs and preferences
- Insurance information
- Payment information

**From Caregivers:**
- Name, address, phone number, email address
- Date of birth, Social Security Number (encrypted)
- Professional licenses and certifications
- Background check information
- Bank account information for payments (encrypted)
- Vehicle and insurance information
- References and employment history

### 2.2 Protected Health Information (PHI)
As a healthcare-related service, we collect, use, and maintain Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined by HIPAA. This includes:
- Medical conditions and diagnoses
- Medications and treatment plans
- Care plans and daily routines
- Emergency medical information
- Healthcare provider information

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- IP address and device information
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Usage data and analytics
- Cookies and similar technologies

---

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

### 3.1 Primary Purposes
We use your information to:
- Match clients with appropriate caregivers
- Facilitate care services and appointments
- Process payments and insurance claims
- Conduct background checks on caregivers
- Communicate about services and appointments
- Provide customer support
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

### 3.2 HIPAA Permitted Uses
As a Business Associate under HIPAA, we may use PHI for:
- Treatment: Coordinating care between clients, caregivers, and healthcare providers
- Payment: Processing payments for services rendered
- Healthcare Operations: Quality assurance, training, and service improvement

### 3.3 Additional Uses (with consent)
With your explicit authorization, we may also use your information for:
- Marketing communications
- Research and analytics
- Sharing with third-party services

---

## 4. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

### 4.1 Security Measures
We implement comprehensive security measures including:

**Technical Safeguards:**
- AES-256 encryption for all PHI at rest
- TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit
- Field-level encryption for sensitive data (SSN, DOB, phone numbers)
- Secure authentication and access controls
- Regular security audits and penetration testing

**Administrative Safeguards:**
- HIPAA Privacy and Security Officers
- Workforce training on privacy and security
- Incident response procedures
- Business Associate Agreements with all vendors

**Physical Safeguards:**
- Secure data centers with SOC 2 compliance
- Access controls to physical facilities
- Device security policies

### 4.2 Data Minimization
We only collect and retain information necessary to provide our services. We regularly review and purge unnecessary data.

### 4.3 Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach involving PHI, we will:
- Notify affected individuals within 60 days
- Report to the Secretary of HHS
- Notify media if breach affects 500+ individuals
- Provide mitigation and credit monitoring services as appropriate

## 5. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

### 5.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Information
CareConnex never sells personal information or PHI to third parties.

### 5.2 Categories of Recipients
We may share information with:

**Caregivers:** Information necessary to provide care services
**Healthcare Providers:** With client authorization for care coordination
**Payment Processors:** Stripe for payment processing
**Background Check Services:** For caregiver verification
**Cloud Service Providers:** Google Cloud (Firebase) for data storage
**SMS/Communication Services:** Twilio for notifications
**Legal Authorities:** When required by law or court order

### 5.3 Business Associate Agreements
All vendors who handle PHI sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) requiring them to:
- Protect PHI in accordance with HIPAA
- Use PHI only as permitted by the agreement
- Report breaches and security incidents
- Return or destroy PHI upon termination

### 5.4 Required Disclosures
We may disclose information without authorization when required by:
- Court orders or subpoenas
- Public health authorities
- Law enforcement (limited circumstances)
- Abuse/neglect reporting (as required by law)

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

### 6.1 HIPAA Rights
Under HIPAA, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of your medical records
- **Amend:** Request corrections to your information
- **Accounting of Disclosures:** Know who we've shared your information with
- **Restrictions:** Request limits on how we use/disclose your information
- **Confidential Communications:** Request alternative means of communication
- **Breach Notification:** Be notified of data breaches

### 6.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)
California residents have additional rights to:
- Know what personal information we collect
- Know how we use and share your information
- Delete personal information (with exceptions)
- Opt-out of "sales" (we don't sell data)
- Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

### 6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights:
- **Email:** privacy@careconnex.com
- **Phone:** 1-800-CARE-NOW
- **Mail:** CareConnex Privacy Office, [Address]
- **Online:** Through your account settings

We will respond to requests within 30 days.

## 7. DATA RETENTION

### 7.1 Retention Periods
- **Active Clients:** Duration of service plus 6 years
- **Inactive Clients:** 6 years from last service date
- **Caregivers:** 6 years from employment end date
- **Financial Records:** 7 years (IRS requirement)
- **Background Checks:** As required by state law

### 7.2 Secure Disposal
When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or destroy it using:
- Cryptographic erasure for digital data
- Secure shredding for physical documents
